Problem brief, "Membership Freeze": Gym members who travel or get injured cancel their membership instead of pausing it, and we lose about 4% of members a year this way. Members should be able to freeze their membership from the app for 1 to 3 months, once per year, and pay no fee while frozen. Front-desk staff must be able to see that a membership is frozen. Success metric: cancellations for travel or injury drop by half within 6 months. Out of scope: freezing family plans.
